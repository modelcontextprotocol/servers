Serve the 2026-07-28 protocol revision alongside the 2025 era (#4852), over stdio (`serveStdio`) and Streamable HTTP (one `/mcp` endpoint: `createMcpHandler` for 2026-07-28 requests, the existing sessionful transport for 2025-era clients). New for 2026-07-28 clients: `server/discover`, multi-round-trip requests (`input_required`) for the elicitation, sampling and roots tools, `subscriptions/listen` change notifications, cache hints (`ttlMs` / `cacheScope`), and the tasks extension (`io.modelcontextprotocol/tasks`, advertised under `capabilities.extensions`), on which `simulate-research-query`, `trigger-sampling-request-async` and `trigger-elicitation-request-async` return. New tools on both eras: `trigger-multi-step-elicitation` (`requestState` across rounds), `trigger-log-messages` (per-request log levels), `trigger-list-changed`, `trigger-resource-update`, and `get-header-params` (`x-mcp-header` and JSON Schema 2020-12 schemas). `toggle-simulated-logging` and `toggle-subscriber-updates` are offered to 2025-era clients only, labelled deprecated with the roots, sampling and logging tools. For 2025-era clients: `get-roots-list` asks the client for its roots on every call; a failed sampling, elicitation or roots request is reported as `Fulfilling input required by 'tools/call' failed: <message>`; a read of a dynamic resource with an invalid id is answered `-32602` with the URI in `data`; and the toggle tools' messages and the subscription acknowledgements no longer name a session id. `gzip-file-as-resource` limits its `name` argument to 255 characters. The HTTP+SSE transport prints a deprecation warning on start.
